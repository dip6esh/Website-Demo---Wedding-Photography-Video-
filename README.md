# Love Story Capture

Wedding Photography & Filmmaking Website — PRD



1. Objective



Create a modern, premium website for a photography and filmmaking business to showcase its work, present its services, introduce the business, and generate enquiries.



The website must be mobile-first and feel like a modern mobile app on phones, while also working properly on desktop and tablet.





---



2. Pages



The website will have 5 pages:



Home



About Us



Services



Our Works



Contact







---



3. Home



The Home page will contain:



Hero section with business name, tagline, photography and enquiry CTA



Short introduction with link to About Us



Services preview



Featured photography



Featured films



Final enquiry/WhatsApp CTA







---



4. About Us



Contains:



Business/studio introduction and story



Photography and filmmaking approach



Team/founder information



Contact CTA







---



5. Services



The website will present these services:



Wedding Photography & Filming



Pre-Wedding Photography & Filming



Baby & Kids Photography



Product Photography



Corporate Photography & Filming



Event Photography & Filming





Each service will have its image, description and enquiry option.



The service structure must allow the business to add or remove services later.





---



6. Our Works



The portfolio will be divided into:



Photography



Categories:



Weddings



Pre-Weddings



Baby & Kids



Products



Corporate



Events





Visitors can browse the photography portfolio by category.



Films



A collection of selected films with:



Thumbnail



Title



Description



YouTube video





YouTube videos should load only when the visitor chooses to watch them, so they don't unnecessarily slow down the website.





---



7. Contact



The Contact page will provide:



Enquiry Form



Name



Phone



Email



Service



Event date



Location



Message





Direct Contact



WhatsApp



Phone



Email





WhatsApp should also be easily accessible while browsing the website.





---



8. Performance



The website will contain many photographs and videos, so it must remain fast.



Images must be optimized and compressed.



Images should load progressively/lazily.



High-resolution originals must not be unnecessarily loaded.



YouTube videos must be loaded on demand.



The initial page load should remain lightweight.







---



9. Core Experience



The website should feel:



Visual → Fast → Mobile-first → App-like → Easy to navigate → Easy to enquire



The photography and films are the primary content of the website, while the overall experience should guide visitors naturally from discovering the business → viewing its work → understanding its services → making an enquiry.
